##### Adapt informed consent

At the start of each survey form, the enumerator reads out an informed consent statement to the participant before any questions are asked. This statement should be adapted to your project — for example to name your organisation, describe how the data will be used, and reflect local ethical requirements.

On this page you can edit the consent statement for each survey form, in each of the languages your team uses. The enumerator instruction (the question label) is shown for context and cannot be changed here.

The text is shown to participants on the data collection device, which supports only limited formatting: **bold**, _italic_ and line breaks. Any other formatting — headings, links, colours, or styling pasted in from elsewhere — is removed when you save.

When you save a change, your team's survey is flagged for an update and the change is compiled and sent to ODK Central the next time the survey is synced.

##### Adapt HDDS hints

The Household Dietary Diversity Score (HDDS) module asks about the different food groups a household has consumed. The wording of each question is standardised so that results stay comparable between projects, but the **hint** text — the short help text shown to enumerators and respondents alongside each question — often needs adapting to use locally familiar examples and terms.

On this page you can review every HDDS question and edit its hint for each of the languages your team uses. The question type, variable name and label cannot be changed here. When you save a hint, your team's survey is flagged for an update and the change is compiled and sent to ODK Central the next time the survey is synced.
